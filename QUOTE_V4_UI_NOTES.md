# Quote V4 - Simplified B2B Pilgrimage Quote UI

Workflow:
1. Travel Agency & Contact
2. Pilgrimage & Requested Dates
3. Group Setup
4. Seasonal Reference Rate
5. Epoch NET Pricing / Internal Costing
6. Agency NET Offer
7. Internal Epoch Profitability
8. Terms, Validity & Send

Commercial policy:
- Quotes are NET to the travel agency.
- The agency controls its resale markup.
- Seasonal rates are internal reference rates, not guaranteed final prices.
- Supplier cost, Epoch markup, and Epoch profit are internal-only.
- Final NET rates are the commercial rates offered to the agency.
